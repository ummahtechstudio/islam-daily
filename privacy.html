🕌 Privacy Policy — Islam Daily

Last Updated: September 20, 2026

Introduction

Islam Daily ("we", "our", "the app") is developed by Ummah Tech Studio. This policy describes, in plain words, exactly what information the app sends off your device, why, where it goes, and how you control it. It was written by checking every network request in the app's source code, and it is updated whenever that changes.

In short

  • Islam Daily has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs. It works mostly offline.
  • Only two things ever reach our own servers, and both are under your control: an optional, anonymous session record (approximate location only — you are asked on first launch and can switch it off in Settings) and any feedback you choose to send.
  • Your precise GPS position is used on your device for prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is sent to a third party in one situation only: when you use the Mosque or Halal finder, the search area goes to the OpenStreetMap Overpass service so it can look up places near you.
  • Everything else — bookmarks, prayer log, memorisation progress, tasbeeh counts, settings, downloaded content — stays on your phone.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Optional anonymous session record ("Anonymous Analytics")

The first time you open the app you are asked whether Islam Daily may record an anonymous session each time it is launched. Nothing is sent unless you tap OK, I understand. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy → Anonymous Analytics.

If you agree, the app sends one record per launch to our own database containing only:

  • Approximate location: your city and country (as reported by your device's built-in reverse geocoder), plus your latitude and longitude rounded to one decimal place — about 10 km. The precise GPS reading is never uploaded. If you have not granted the app location permission, this part is simply left empty — the app never asks for the permission for this purpose.
  • Device and app: platform (Android or iOS), operating-system version, device model name (for example "Pixel 7") and the app version.
  • Time: when the record arrived.

The record contains no name, e-mail, account, device ID, advertising ID or any other identifier. No identifier is stored, so we have no way to tell which records came from the same phone. We use these records for one purpose: to understand which regions and devices the app serves, so we can prioritise languages, calculation methods and testing.

1.2 Feedback you send

If you use Settings → Send Feedback, we receive what you type: your message, category, optional star rating, an optional name and an optional e-mail address (both may be left blank), together with the app version, platform and device model so we can reproduce problems. If you attach a screenshot, the image is resized and uploaded too.

Please do not include anything sensitive in a screenshot. Uploaded screenshots are stored in a storage bucket that can be reached by URL without a login. If you give an e-mail address we use it only to reply to you.

1.3 Information that stays on your device

The following is stored only on your phone and is never sent to us: bookmarks and favourites, prayer log and streaks, Hifz (memorisation) progress, tasbeeh counts, reading position, notification and prayer-time settings, chosen city, theme and language, downloaded Quran / hadith / dua / audio content, and the app's internal usage counters (screens opened, features used). These counters exist only locally and are not uploaded. All of it is removed when you uninstall the app, or when you use Settings → Storage & Data → Reset All Settings.

1.4 What we do not collect

  • ❌ No accounts, logins or passwords — the app has none.
  • ❌ No name, e-mail or phone number, unless you type them into the feedback form.
  • ❌ No device identifier, advertising ID or push-notification token (all reminders are scheduled locally on your phone).
  • ❌ No reading history, bookmarks or in-app behaviour.
  • ❌ No contacts, photos (other than a screenshot you choose to attach), microphone audio or camera images.
  • ❌ No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs of any kind.

2. How your location is used

Location permission is optional. If you decline it you can still choose a city manually for prayer times.

Prayer times & Hijri calendarCalculated on your device (adhan-js library, Umm al-Qura calendar). Your position is not sent to any server for this.
Qibla directionCalculated on your device from your position and the coordinates of the Kaaba. No server is contacted.
City name shown on Home / Prayer TimesYour device's built-in reverse geocoder turns coordinates into a city name. On Android this is provided by Google Play services and on iOS by Apple; the request is made by the operating system under its own privacy policy.

This is the only third-party service that receives your position from the app, and only while you use these screens. When you tap "Directions", only the destination's coordinates are handed to your maps app.
Anonymous session record (optional)Rounded to about 10 km before it is sent — see 1.1.

4. Where data is stored and for how long

  • Session records and feedback are stored in our database and file storage hosted by Supabase, protected by HTTPS in transit, and accessible only to the Islam Daily development team.
  • Retention: session records are anonymous and are kept in aggregate for as long as they are useful for understanding where the app is used. Feedback is kept while we work on the issue it describes and for reference afterwards. There is no automatic deletion; we delete on request (see 5).
  • Content (Quran, hadith, duas, audio) is stored on your device once downloaded and can be cleared at any time from Settings → Storage & Data.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Turn the session record off: Settings → Privacy → Anonymous Analytics. Declining on first launch has the same effect. Nothing else in the app depends on it.
  • Revoke permissions (location, camera, notifications) at any time in your phone's settings. The app keeps working; features that need a permission will ask again or offer a manual alternative.
  • Delete local data: Settings → Storage & Data → Reset All Settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Delete feedback you sent: e-mail us with the approximate date and the message text (or the e-mail address you entered) and we will delete the row and any screenshot. Because session records contain no identifier we cannot single out "your" records, but you can stop new ones at any time.
  • Access, correction, objection, complaint: if you are in the EU/EEA, UK or another region with data-protection rights, you can ask us what we hold, ask for it to be corrected or erased, or object to its use, by e-mailing us. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

6. Permissions the app may ask for

  • Location (optional): prayer times, Qibla, Mosque/Halal finders, city name, and the optional session record described in 1.1.
  • Camera (optional): the augmented-reality Qibla view only. No photo or video is captured, stored or sent.
  • Photos (optional): only to pick a screenshot to attach to feedback. Only the image you select is uploaded.
  • Notifications / exact alarms: adhan and prayer-time reminders, daily hadith and other reminders you enable. On Android the app uses exact alarms so reminders fire at the prayer time; it re-schedules them after a restart (boot-completed permission).
  • Internet: to download content, stream audio and send feedback.

7. Children's privacy

Islam Daily is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The only information that reaches us is the anonymous session record (if enabled) and feedback that is deliberately sent; if a child has sent us personal information through the feedback form, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All network requests use HTTPS. Data on our backend is accessible only to the development team. Local data is kept in the app's private storage on your device.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, this page and the matching text inside the app (the first-launch privacy prompt, Settings → Privacy, and Sources & Credits) are updated together, and the "Last Updated" date above changes. Significant changes will also be pointed out inside the app.
