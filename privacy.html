🕌 Privacy Policy — Islam Daily

Last Updated: April 27, 2026

Introduction

Islam Daily ("we", "our", "the app") is developed by Ummah Tech Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

We respect your privacy. Islam Daily is designed to work primarily offline and we collect minimal information.

Information We Collect

1. Information You Provide

  • Feedback: If you submit feedback through the app, we receive your message, optional name, and optional email. This is stored securely in our Supabase database.
  • Bookmarks: Your bookmarks (favorite hadiths, duas, ayahs) are stored locally on your device only. We do not collect this data.
  • Settings: Your preferences (theme, prayer times calculation method, etc.) are stored locally on your device only.

2. Information Collected Automatically

  • Location (Optional): If you grant location permission, we use your device's GPS to:
    • Calculate accurate prayer times for your location
    • Show the Qibla direction
    • Find nearby mosques
    Your location is processed on your device. We do not store or transmit your location to our servers.
  • Device Information: When you submit feedback, we collect basic device info (OS type and app version) to help diagnose issues.

3. Information We DO NOT Collect

  • ❌ We do not collect your name, email, or phone number unless you voluntarily submit it via feedback
  • ❌ We do not track your reading habits, bookmarks, or app usage
  • ❌ We do not use cookies or web tracking
  • ❌ We do not share your information with advertisers
  • ❌ We do not have user accounts or login systems

Third-Party Services

Islam Daily uses the following third-party services to provide content:

  • Aladhan API — for prayer times calculation (sends only city/coordinates)
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass API) — for finding nearby mosques (sends only your coordinates and search radius)
  • Cloudflare R2 — for hosting hadith content (no user data sent)
  • Supabase — for storing feedback submissions only

These services may collect basic technical information per their own privacy policies. We do not share any personal information with them.

Data Storage

  • On your device: Bookmarks, settings, and reading progress are stored locally using AsyncStorage. They never leave your phone.
  • On our servers: Only feedback messages (if you submit them). We may keep these to improve the app.

Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Location: For prayer times, Qibla, and mosque finder. Optional.
  • Notifications: For Adhan reminders and daily hadith notifications. Optional.
  • Internet: To download Quran/Hadith content and submit feedback.

You can revoke any permission at any time through your device's settings.

Children's Privacy

Islam Daily is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption for all network requests. Local data is stored in your device's secure storage.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Stop using the app at any time
  • Clear all local data via Settings → Storage & Data → Reset All Settings
  • Request deletion of any feedback you submitted (contact us with the message details)
  • Ask questions about your data (contact us)

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. The "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Significant changes will be communicated within the app.
