## Executive summary

**The app is in genuinely good shape.** A 96-agent deep audit surfaced 187 findings, but after independent re-reading of every correctness/robustness claim, **zero criticals survived**, only **5 are high-severity**, and **94 things that looked risky were confirmed solid** (including 10 claims a second agent refuted on re-read). The recent consolidation work all held up:

- **Prayer/Hijri engine is single-source.** No second prayer engine or duplicate Hijri path leaks anywhere; `getHijriParts` (Umm al-Qura) and the `adhan`-based `computePrayerTimes` are the only sources. The only dead leftover is two unused `PrayerTimes`/`HijriDate` interfaces in `api.ts` (harmless).
- **Quran render perf pass held.** Verse rows and Mushaf pages are memoized; no re-render regression was found. Virtualization over the 6 236-ayah corpus is correct.
- **Dead-code removal held.** No resurrected dead controls or duplicate engines.

The real weak spots cluster into seven themes:

1. **Location/Qibla fallback semantics** — the GPS-denied fallback uses the Kaaba's *own* coordinates, so a user who declines location is shown a confident-looking but meaningless "Qibla 0.0°"; a stale-closure bug can also overwrite a good cached location. (Religious-data.)
2. **Notification delivery completeness** — no `setNotificationHandler` (foreground prayer/test notifications show nothing) and no cold-start tap handler (tapping a daily/Friday reminder from a killed app drops the deep-link). These hit the *most common* notification paths.
3. **One visibly-broken feature** — tasbeeh **Timer auto-count mode** freezes after the first tick (stale-closure).
4. **Religious-data display nuances** — juz number is wrong on 2 boundary pages (verified against `quran.json`); Verse-of-the-Day renders Urdu in the English slot; tajweed over-colours qalqala and misses cross-word ikhfa; zakat-due displays rounded *down*.
5. **Hung-spinner UX** — mosque & halal finders await `getCurrentPositionAsync` with no timeout, so a weak GPS fix can hang the screen with no retry.
6. **Audio lifecycle** — the (feature-flagged-off) general `AudioPlayerContext` can leak/duplicate a native `Sound` on rapid taps/unmount, and doesn't auto-advance playlists; the Quran audio context (live) is solid by contrast.
7. **Latent data-drift bugs** — a cluster of real bugs in the Supabase remote-content write paths (dua category titles, dropped `title_en/ur`, names guard) that are currently **masked** by the coverage guards and the stale-pilot table, but will bite the day the remote tables are repopulated.

A large share of findings are **latent** — guarded by the coverage guards or behind feature flags — real but not currently user-reachable. They are marked as such so they can be scheduled rather than rushed. The honest bottom line: nothing here is on fire, the religious-data engines are sound, and the fix list below is about closing edge cases and polishing — not rescuing a broken app.
